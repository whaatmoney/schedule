Rules for every post
These apply in every channel. Format errors count against the tech.

Use the official format. Do not change it. Feb 2026

Every post uses the department format on this page, field for field. No shortcuts, no personal versions. Wrong format is counted against the tech.

One post per job per department

The main post is the cover post for the whole job. Line items, boxes, pauses, and inspections go in comments under it. Do not start a second main post for the same job in the same department unless the job comes back for reprocessing.

Hashtag the WO on every post

Add #WOxxxxx (no spaces, five digits) and write the WO number in the text too. A search on the WO must pull the full trail from receiving to shipping. Purchased materials use #PURCHASING.

Start time in the post. End time by editing the same post.

Post the start time when you begin. When you finish, edit the original post to add the end time. Do not post the end time as a new post or comment. HH:MM am/pm.

Special Instructions: write them in your own words Feb 26, 2026

"Yes" or "No" is not enough. Rewrite what the special instruction means for your step. If it does not apply to your operation, say why, not just "N/A". Wrong interpretation flags a training need and reflects on the supervisor.

General Notes must always be filled in

Never leave General Notes blank. Include who processed, special instructions, verified quantity, and any cross-department handoff. Edit it after all inspections are complete.

Router photo at every handoff Aug 26, 2026

Pre-clean, cleanroom, and packaging techs photograph their completed router before handing off. Inspectors photograph the stamped router before it leaves them. Full page, flat, readable. One photo per section. Both pages if the router has a back page. Missing router photos have led to written warnings.

Traceability label in every photo

Every part photo shows the traceability label: company, WO, line, part ID, qty, batch, department. If the label is not in the frame, the photo does not count.

Verified quantity, not assumed

Qty in the post is a count you made and it matches the traveler. Note any extra or missing pieces. Pre-clean inspection posts must have a verified quantity.

Reprocessing gets a new post and a new router Sep 4, 2026

When parts are rejected and sent back for recleaning, the supervisor documents the rejection on Viva Engage, identifies the exact pieces, and the reprocessing gets its own post and router.

NCRs are reported in Teams, not Viva Engage Aug 19, 2026

The Viva Engage NCR channel is retired. Report and disposition NCRs in the Teams NCR - CA channel. Viva Engage keeps the photo proof and the department post. Tag the NCR number in the department post so they link.

Tag people with @ so it links

When you need a response, @mention the person. If several techs worked one job, @mention each name. A plain typed name does not notify anyone.

Photos may be shared with customers

Clear, in focus, well lit, oriented. Packaging inspection photos represent QPC's work and are sometimes sent to the customer as-is.

Edited a post and it disappeared? Log it.

If editing a post deletes it, write it up in the incident log and report it. Do not quietly re-post.

Post before the job leaves your hands

The post is made at your inspection point, after your step is complete and before the next operator touches the job. Batching posts at end of day breaks the timeline.

Pause, continue, finish: one comment each

Stopping mid-job? Comment PAUSE AT 3:18PM. Picking it back up? Comment CONTINUE AT 7:13AM. Done? Comment COMPLETE or FINISHED AT 10:50AM and edit the end time into the main post. This is how every department already does it in the export.

Washington site posts

WA posts carry the #QPCWA tag on the first line and the WO number ends in WA (42665WA). Everything else is the same format as CA.

Photo standards
If it cannot be read, it does not count.

Every part photo

  • Traceability label in the frame: company, WO, line, part ID, qty, batch, department.
  • In focus, well lit, no glare over the label.
  • Part oriented so markings and surfaces can be judged.
  • Before and after your process, at your inspection point.

Router photo Aug 26, 2026

  • Full page, flat, readable. Both pages if there is a back page.
  • Taken after your section is complete and before the job leaves your hands.
  • One photo per section. If the job comes back to you, take it again.
  • Added to the same Viva post as your part photos, WO number in the text.
  • Applies to every job including expedites, until the operations router system is live.

Receiving inspection

  • Part with ruler for dimensions, one per line item.
  • Close-up of serial, lot, and heat markings.
  • How the job arrived: trays, containers, packaging.

Packaging and shipping

  • Each packaging step separately: nylon bag, poly bag, completed package.
  • Seal close-ups. Humidity indicator and desiccant if used.
  • Box contents with labels, then box exterior with dimensions and labels.
  • These photos may go to the customer. Treat them that way.

NCR photos

  • Show the discrepancy clearly. Scale or ruler if size matters.
  • Identifiers legible in every NCR photo.
  • Before and after photos on any rework.

Where real posts fall short

  • Median photo count on a main post in the export is 1. Inspection is 2. The guidelines ask for 3 or more per line item.
  • Router photos only became a requirement Aug 26, 2026, after the export, so none of the 4,522 posts show one. Expect every post to now.
  • Special Instructions is still a bare YES in most WA posts. CA cleanroom and packaging inspection already write it out.

What gets a post rejected

  • Finish time with no finish photo.
  • Label out of frame or unreadable.
  • One overall photo standing in for line-item photos.
  • Router photo missing, cropped, or only one page of two.
Where things go
